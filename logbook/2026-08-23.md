# 2026-08-23 — CPU cooler swap: NH-D15 G2 → NL-LC1-42 (420 mm AIO)

Hardware change outside the paper's measurement path — the benchmarks are GPU-bound
and the CPU is not the bottleneck. Full analysis lives in `bom/cooling-test/report.md`;
this entry records the hardware boundary and the outcome only. The Cooling row in
`bom/readme.md` was updated the same day.

## A/B/C test result (`bom/cooling-test/`)

Procedure per run: 10 min idle → 40 min stress-ng `matrixprod` (32 threads) → 10 min
cooldown, sensors sampled every 5 s. Comparison metric is ΔT above ambient, because
the runs differ in ambient temperature.

| run | date | variant | ambient [°C] | ΔT load [K] | Tctl max [°C] | clock [MHz] | bogo-ops/s | CPU_Opt [RPM] | status |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A `noctua` | 2026-07-31 | NH-D15 G2 | 24.5 | 56.1 | 81.2 | 5042 | 25 942 | 1436 | valid |
| B `lc142` | 2026-08-23 00:35 | LC1-42, radiator fans STOPPED | 23.5 | 59.9 | 86.4 | 5019 | 25 974 | **0** | **INVALID** |
| C `lc142-fanC` | 2026-08-23 08:33 | LC1-42, fans running | 24.0 | 56.8 | 81.2 | 5017 | 25 929 | 2162 | valid |

- **Under load it is a tie (C vs A):** the 0.7 K ΔT difference sits below the noise of
  a single run, and `Tctl max` is identical to the decimal (81.2 °C).
- **Run B is invalid:** the `CPU_Opt` channel reported 0 RPM in every sample across
  61 minutes, including 40 minutes of stress-ng — the radiator fans were not turning.
  The earlier verdict "AIO worse by 3.8 K" was an artefact of absent airflow, not a
  property of the cooler. B is retained in the report as a negative control.
- **Noise:** 53–55 dBA (D15) vs 51.2–53 dBA (LC) at 50 cm, SPL Meter app, uncalibrated
  microphone — the relative difference is credible, the absolute values are not.
- **Limits:** n = 1 per variant, different days and different kernels (7.0.0-28 / -29 / -30).

## Methodological lesson

A zero on a channel that is capable of reporting is a **measurement, not missing data** —
the same channel read 1436 RPM with the Noctua installed. Writing `NA` instead of zero in
`snap()` (`cooling_ab_test.sh`), introduced so as not to fake a missing sensor, masked the
real signal and cost one invalidated run. The fix — distinguishing "channel absent from
`sensors` output" (`NA`) from "channel present and reporting 0" (`0`), plus a warning when
zero appears under load — is described in report.md § Dług.
