## Side finding — runner kill discipline

`nohup vllm serve ... &` `$!` captures the wrapper-bash PID, not the vLLM
PID; SIGTERM does not propagate. Mitigated post-hoc with
`pgrep -f 'vllm serve.*--port N'`. Future runner must probe the real vLLM
PID, not `$!`. Kill discipline preserved — no `pkill -f`.
