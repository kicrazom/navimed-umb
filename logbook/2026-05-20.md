## Batch sanity — 3 PASS / 11 FAIL

classifies ok / degenerate / parse_fail to separate broken inference from
infra failure. `kill_port.sh` (new) provides setsid-isolated port cleanup,
replacing ad-hoc `pkill -f`. The 11 FAILs have disjoint causes:

- **5× PLLuM-70B** — physical HIP OOM (~132 GB BF16 > 64 GB VRAM)

- **1× qwen3.5-9b** — vLLM expects `preprocessor_config.json` (multimodal)
- **1× bielik-11b-v30-instruct-awq** — tokenizer instantiation fail
