## Engineering finding — enforce_eager mandatory beyond hybrid attention

During the Phase 1 envelope, the `awq_tp1_max2048_util090_graphs` config
(`enforce_eager=False`) hard-segfaulted inside
`libhsa-runtime64.so.1.18.70200`. This extends METHODOLOGY §3.2 — the eager
requirement is not specific to hybrid-attention models (Qwen 3.5/3.6); on
gfx1201, **even Mistral-based models require `enforce_eager=True`** because
the CUDA-graph capture path crashes in the ROCm HSA runtime. Recommendation:
§3.2 errata for v1.1 broadening "hybrid attention models" to "all models on
gfx1201 under vLLM 0.19.0+rocm721 + PyTorch 2.10".
