## Why now is the clean window

- **Phase 1 results are firmware-independent.** The envelope quantities
  (load success, VRAM footprint, KV-cache capacity, max-concurrency) are
  determined by the model–quantization–vLLM/ROCm stack, not by BIOS/AGESA.
  Phase 1 stays valid under 2202.
- **Phase 2 (firmware-sensitive: throughput / latency / thermal / power)
  will run entirely under one current, stable firmware (2202).** Better
  than measuring Phase 2 on the older 1715 and having to defend an outdated
  AGESA to a reviewer.
- The only firmware-sensitive Phase-1 quantity is the single-prompt
  `sanity_throughput_tok_s` (memory-bandwidth-bound baseline) — diagnostic,
  not a headline number.
