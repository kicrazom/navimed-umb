## Thermals + power — engineering envelope (PUBLIC §11.1)

Across all three sweeps, GPU thermals stayed well below the gfx1201 throttle threshold:

- Junction peak (per-GPU max across sweep): ~92–94 °C (R9700 limit ~110 °C; ~16 °C headroom).
- Edge / memory: 62–67 °C / 30–66 °C.
- Package power per GPU under load: ~290–320 W steady, transient dips during N transitions.
- Idle after `kill_port` cleanup between models: junction returns to 31–34 °C in <1 min.
- No thermal throttling observed in any sweep.
